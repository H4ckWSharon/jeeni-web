What is Jeeni?

Jeeni is an AI companion that studies alongside you. It reads from the textbooks you actually use, learns how you like to be taught, and explains every idea until it clicks.

  • ✦Knows your syllabus (CBSE, SCERT, NCERT)

  • ✦Answers in your preferred language
  • ✦No internet needed for textbook content (offline RAG)
